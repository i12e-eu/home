3. Website Visits and Correspondence

Website visits involve technical data such as IP addresses, browser information, page requests, and error or security records. Hosting and delivery providers use this information to serve the website, resolve faults, and prevent misuse. Requests for shared design assets and Google Fonts also disclose your IP address and browser information to their providers.

When you contact us, we use your name, contact details, messages, attachments, and related correspondence information to respond and follow up. Financial administration may also involve contact details, invoices, and payment records. Providing enquiry information is voluntary, but we need enough detail and a way to contact you to reply.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The website stores display and reading preferences in your browser’s local storage. They have no programmed expiry; you can remove them by clearing the site’s data in your browser. Cloudflare may use security cookies or similar mechanisms to protect the website.

We do not use advertising or behavioural analytics, or track whether you open our emails or click their links.

5. One-Off Donations

If you donate through a Mollie Payment Link, you enter payment information with Mollie or the bank or payment provider handling your chosen method. They process the payment details, transaction information, and any identity or contact details needed to complete the payment. Mollie also processes technical information such as your IP address and browser information.

We receive donation records, including the amount and payment status. Depending on the payment method, these may include your name, contact details, bank-account information, or masked card details. We use them to record and reconcile donations and handle enquiries, refunds, or disputes. We do not receive full card numbers, card security codes, or online-banking credentials through this checkout.

Where contact details are available, we may send a personal thank-you or acknowledgement. With your separate consent, we may also publish your name to acknowledge your donation publicly. Public acknowledgement is optional and does not affect your donation. Donating does not give consent to unrelated marketing.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Website operation: our legitimate interests in providing a reliable website, resolving faults, and preventing misuse (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Correspondence and administration: our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and managing ordinary administration (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Donation payments: performance of the donation agreement, where processing is needed to carry out an individual’s donation or related transaction corrections (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Donation communications and disputes: our legitimate interests in sending personal acknowledgements, communicating with organisational donors’ representatives, preventing fraud, and handling disputes or legal claims (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Public donor acknowledgement: your separate consent to publication (Article 6(1)(a)).
  • Required records and rights requests: compliance with accounting and data-protection obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

7. Providers and Other Recipients

  • GitHub Pages: hosting for the website and shared assets.
  • Cloudflare: website delivery, security, and technical reporting.
  • Google Fonts: delivery of website fonts.
  • Email and accounting service providers: correspondence and financial administration.
  • Mollie: donor personal data are shared with Mollie and its affiliates to process donation payments. Mollie acts as an independent controller for payment processing, fraud prevention, and related financial and legal obligations.

Payments also involve banks and the selected payment-method providers. Providers may process some information for their own security or legal obligations under their published terms.

We may share relevant information with professional advisers or authorities where required by law or necessary to address legal claims. Donor names published with consent are available to the public.

8. International Processing

Our providers operate internationally, and personal data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in Switzerland and the United States. Switzerland is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision.

Published provider arrangements include the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and European Commission standard contractual clauses where applicable. Use our privacy contact details for information about the safeguards relevant to your data or to obtain a copy.

9. How Long Information Is Kept

We review the information we hold and use the following retention criteria:

  • Correspondence: while needed to answer an enquiry, complete follow-up, maintain a relevant relationship, or resolve outstanding legal or dispute matters.
  • Required accounting records, including donation records: generally seven years after they cease to have current administrative relevance, or longer where required by law. See the Dutch Tax Administration’s guidance.
  • Public acknowledgements: while the acknowledgement remains relevant and your consent remains in place.
  • Technical records: according to the providers’ retention criteria for website delivery, troubleshooting, security, and legal requirements.

Mollie retains payment information according to its own legal obligations and processing purposes, as explained in its privacy statement.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or portability of eligible personal data. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we can demonstrate compelling grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or need the data for legal claims. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Send privacy questions or requests to privacy@infrastructure.eu. Requests are normally free. If we reasonably doubt your identity, we may ask for information needed to verify it. We respond without undue delay and within one month. Complex or numerous requests may require up to two additional months; we will explain any extension within the first month. If a legal exception prevents us from fulfilling a request, we will explain why and how you can challenge the decision.

You can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority, or the competent authority where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact us first.

Mollie’s privacy statement explains how to exercise your rights in relation to its payment processing, including when you can request human review of automated fraud decisions.

11. Changes and Contact

We will revise this policy when changes to our use of personal data or applicable law require it. The date above shows when it was last updated.

For questions or requests, contact privacy@infrastructure.eu.
