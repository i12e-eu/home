1. What this policy covers

The foundation works to make European digital infrastructure easier to discover, understand, and compare. The current website introduces this purpose and links to planning documents.

This notice covers the informational website, correspondence, and related administration. It also explains how we will handle personal information if you donate through a Mollie Payment Link when these links become available. The planned comparison platform and research functionality are not current website services. We will review and update the relevant privacy information before introducing new activities involving personal data. The informational site currently has no accounts, submission forms, or newsletter signup; the donation checkout described below is hosted by Mollie.

3. Website visits and browser storage

Delivering a webpage involves technical information about the request. Our hosting and delivery providers, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, process information such as IP addresses, requested pages and resources, request times, browser information, and security or error information. Referring addresses may also be included, depending on the browser and request. GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

Cloudflare also sends instructions that allow supporting browsers to report network failures. These reports help identify connection problems and maintain website reliability.

The landing page loads styles, scripts, and artwork from our shared design site at design.i12e.eu, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. It also retrieves fonts from Google Fonts. These requests disclose the visitor’s IP address and relevant browser and request information to the service delivering the resource. This privacy document embeds its own fonts and styles and does not load those external resources.

When you choose a light or dark appearance on the landing page, it saves that choice in your browser’s local storage. The saved value is a display preference, not a visitor identifier. It has no programmed expiry and remains until it is replaced or removed, including when you clear the site’s data in your browser or the browser clears it.

Cloudflare may use security cookies or similar mechanisms when its protective features apply to a request. Their use depends on the protection involved; see Cloudflare’s explanation of its cookies. We do not use advertising trackers or a separate behavioural-analytics service. Hosting providers still process technical records and provide operational and security information.

4. Correspondence and administration

If you contact us, we handle your email address, name and other contact details you provide, your message and attachments, and associated information such as message headers, dates, and delivery details. We use this information to understand your enquiry, respond, and manage follow-up.

Related administration can involve contact and organisation details, invoices, and payment information contained in the records you send us. We use Proton Mail for correspondence and Odoo for accounting and administration.

Providing enquiry information is voluntary. Without enough information about your request and a way to reach you, we may be unable to help or reply. Please limit messages to relevant information and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal details.

We do not currently send a newsletter or measure whether recipients open our correspondence or click its links. Ordinary email delivery and security processing still takes place.

5. One-off donations

If you donate through a Mollie Payment Link, the link takes you to Mollie’s hosted checkout. You enter payment information there or with the bank or payment provider for your chosen method. These donations are one-off payments: they do not create recurring charges or subscriptions, and we do not issue donation invoices.

Mollie processes payment and transaction details, your IP address, and browser and device information. Depending on the payment method and information supplied, it may also process your name, address, email address, or telephone number. Required details depend on the selected method; without the information needed for that method, the payment cannot be completed.

We can access the donation’s amount, currency, date, reference, status, and payment method, together with relevant refund or dispute information. Method-dependent payer details can include a name, bank-account details, contact information, or masked card details. Through this hosted payment-link flow, we do not receive full card numbers, card security codes, or online-banking login credentials. See Mollie’s explanation of method-specific payment details.

Our donation transaction records are kept in Mollie and bank statements. We use them to identify and administer donations, reconcile payments, and handle related enquiries, refunds, or disputes. Where contact details are available to us, we may send a personal thank-you or acknowledgement. Donating does not subscribe you to a mailing list or give consent to unrelated marketing.

6. Why we process information

The GDPR requires a legal basis for using personal information. For the activities in this policy:

  • Website delivery, presentation, and security: our legitimate interests in making the foundation’s information available and readable, maintaining reliable access, investigating faults, and protecting the site against misuse (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Enquiries and related follow-up: our legitimate interests in communicating with people who contact the foundation and handling its ordinary administration (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Executing an individual’s donation: performing the donation agreement you request, to the extent processing is necessary to receive the contribution and make related transaction corrections (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Other donation administration: our legitimate interests in acknowledging support, communicating with representatives of organisational donors, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Mandatory accounting records and data-rights requests: compliance with applicable legal obligations, including Dutch recordkeeping requirements for donations and obligations under data protection law (Article 6(1)(c)).

Legitimate interests must be weighed against the rights and interests of the people concerned. You can object to processing on this basis as explained below. Visiting the website, sending an email, or reading this policy does not itself provide consent to other uses of your information.

7. Who receives information

Access to correspondence and administrative records is limited to authorised people handling enquiries or administration. Advisers receive information only when needed for their work. Our service providers have the following roles:

GitHub Pages
Hosting the website and shared design assets, including technical delivery and security processing. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.
Cloudflare
Website delivery, network protection, and reliability reporting. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and data processing terms.
Google Fonts
Delivering fonts requested by the landing page. See Google’s privacy policy.
Proton Mail
Receiving, storing, and sending correspondence, including message and delivery information. See the Proton Mail privacy policy.
Odoo
Accounting and administration, including relevant contact details and financial records. See Odoo’s privacy policy.
Mollie
Handling one-off donation payments, preventing fraud, and meeting its legal obligations as a payment provider. Mollie acts as an independent controller for this processing, while the foundation remains responsible for its own use of donation records. See Mollie’s privacy statement and User Agreement.

Donation payments also involve banks and the selected payment-method providers. Mollie may share relevant information with service providers supporting payment processing and fraud prevention, and with authorities where legally required, as described in its privacy statement. Our donation transaction records are not transferred to Odoo.

Providers process information needed to supply their services. Some also process technical or service information for their own security, administration, or legal responsibilities, as described in their notices; they are not acting solely on our instructions for every activity.

We may disclose relevant information where the law requires it, or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy arrangements.

8. How long information is kept

We review the personal information we hold and remove it when it is no longer needed for its purpose, taking account of remaining obligations or claims. The criteria differ by record:

  • Correspondence: whether the enquiry and any follow-up are complete, whether an ongoing relationship still requires the record, and whether an unresolved request, dispute, or legal obligation requires continued retention. These criteria also apply to donor enquiries and acknowledgement correspondence.
  • Accounting and supporting records, including donation transactions and reconciliation records: the applicable Dutch statutory retention period, generally seven years after a record ceases to have current administrative relevance. Longer periods apply where required by law. A donation does not make every related message or optional contact detail part of the mandatory accounting record. See the Dutch Tax Administration’s retention guidance for foundations.
  • Technical and security records: the providers’ applicable retention criteria for delivery, troubleshooting, security, and legal requirements. We do not export or maintain a separate archive of visitor logs. Retention varies by provider and record; their privacy notices are linked above.

Mollie separately retains information for its own statutory obligations and, where no statutory period applies, for as long as needed for its processing purposes. Its retention requirements may prevent deletion of some payment records following a request. See Mollie’s retention explanation.

Removing a record from an active service does not necessarily remove its backup copies immediately. Proton reports backup retention of up to 30 days; Odoo states that deleted data may remain in its backups for up to 12 months. These are provider backup cycles, not the retention periods for our active correspondence or accounting records.

9. International processing

Some processing takes place outside the European Economic Area (EEA). The foundation’s location in the Netherlands and its European purpose do not mean that all of its providers process data only within the EEA.

Proton is based in Switzerland and states that Proton Mail server data is stored in Switzerland, Germany, or Norway. Switzerland benefits from a European Commission adequacy decision, which recognises its level of data protection for transfers from the EEA.

GitHub, Cloudflare, and Google operate internationally, including in the United States. Their published arrangements describe reliance on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and European Commission standard contractual clauses for relevant other transfers. The scope depends on the provider and processing involved. Details are in GitHub’s transfer information, Cloudflare’s data processing terms, and Google’s transfer frameworks.

Our Odoo cloud database uses the European hosting region. Odoo’s published arrangements also describe access by international support staff and, for some European-hosted databases, backups in Canada. Odoo describes standard contractual clauses for transfers involving international staff access. European production hosting therefore does not mean that every related activity stays in the EEA. See Odoo’s data-location and transfer information.

Mollie states that third parties involved in its services may process personal information outside the EEA. It describes applicable transfer arrangements, including European Commission standard contractual clauses where relevant. See the cross-border transfer section of Mollie’s privacy statement.

You can contact hello@infrastructure.eu for more information about the arrangements relevant to your information, including how to obtain a copy of applicable safeguards.

10. Your rights and complaints

Subject to the conditions in data protection law, you can ask to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, have information erased, or restrict its processing. These rights also apply to donor information; required accounting records may need to be retained despite an erasure request.

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will stop this processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or need it to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. You also have a right to data portability where its conditions apply: it concerns information you provided that is processed automatically on the basis of consent or a contract.

Send a request to hello@infrastructure.eu or write to the postal address above. Requests are normally free. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask for information necessary to confirm it; we do not routinely require a copy of an identity document.

For donation information handled by the foundation, use the contact details above. For information Mollie processes as an independent controller, you can contact privacy@mollie.com directly. Mollie explains its own handling of rights requests in its privacy statement.

We will respond without undue delay and within one month of receiving your request. Where the complexity or number of requests justifies an extension, the deadline may be extended by up to two further months; we will explain the extension within the first month. If a legal exception prevents us from acting on a request, we will explain the reason and how you can challenge it.

You may complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority, or to the competent supervisory authority where you habitually live, work, or believe an infringement occurred. You do not have to contact us before making a complaint.

The foundation does not make decisions about individuals solely by automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Mollie separately describes automated checks to detect fraud and financial crime, and rights to human review and to contest qualifying automated decisions, in the automated decision-making section of its privacy statement.

11. Security

The website uses HTTPS to protect information in transit between your browser and the service. Access to correspondence and accounting records is limited to authorised people who need it for enquiries or administration. These measures reduce risk, but cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, accessed without permission, or otherwise compromised.

If you believe personal information handled by us has been exposed or misused, please contact hello@infrastructure.eu.
