European Digital Infrastructure Foundation

We are an independent nonprofit documenting the capabilities, ownership, and dependencies of European digital infrastructure. We help policymakers, organisations, and individuals make informed choices, working to advance European digital sovereignty for the public good. Donations support independent research, open data, and comparison tools, with the resulting knowledge free for everyone to use and share.
