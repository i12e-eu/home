2. Donations

Donations are voluntary gifts supporting our purpose: advancing European digital sovereignty for the public benefit through independent research and openly reusable knowledge about digital infrastructure. They provide general support, including reasonable operating and administrative costs, and are not earmarked for a particular project. A donation does not purchase goods or services or commit you to further payments.

A donation does not guarantee a particular project outcome or delivery date. We make no claim that donations are tax-deductible.

3. Payments and Refunds

One-off donation payments are processed through Mollie Payment Links. Check the amount and currency shown at checkout before confirming your payment. The foundation is the recipient of your donation; Mollie provides the payment service. Please contact finance@infrastructure.eu about donation questions or refund requests.

We may decline donations that are incompatible with our purpose or articles of association. We will return payments where required by law. A change of mind does not, by itself, create a right to a refund.

If you believe a payment was mistaken, duplicated, or unauthorised, contact finance@infrastructure.eu promptly, giving the payment date, amount, and reference where available. For suspected unauthorised payments, also notify your bank or payment provider promptly. These terms do not limit any applicable refund or payment-dispute rights. You may use the same email address for donation-related complaints.

4. Independence and Funding Transparency

Financial support gives no preferential treatment or right to determine research findings, approve publication, or prevent publication.

We publish the identities of organisations that fund us, together with the amounts, dates, purposes, material conditions, and funding agreements. Information may be withheld, redacted, or delayed only where necessary for legal obligations, privacy, third-party rights, or a specific, substantiated security risk.

We publish individual donors’ names only with their consent. Their donations remain included in our published financial totals.

5. Privacy

Our privacy policy explains how we handle website, correspondence, and donation information. Mollie’s privacy statement explains its payment processing.
